Factor Calculations

Valuation

liquidity_score = clamp(25 + daily_volume / 200000) reversal_score = clamp(50 - one_day_return*1200 - intraday_return*400) valuation_score = clamp(reversal_score*0.75 + liquidity_score*0.25)

If fundamentals exist, valuation blends in profitability and leverage:

valuation = clamp(valuation*0.65 + profitability*0.20 + leverage*0.15)

Quality

quality_score = clamp(30 + liquidity*0.7 - abs(intraday_return - one_day_return)*900)

With fundamentals, quality also incorporates profitability, cash generation, and asset efficiency.

quality = clamp(quality*0.45 + profitability*0.20 + cash_generation*0.20 + asset_efficiency*0.15)

Growth

trend_score = clamp(50 + one_day_return*1800 + intraday_return*700) growth_score = clamp(25 + trend_score*0.75 + max(one_day_return, 0)*500)

Fundamentals and macro can raise/lower growth.

growth = clamp(growth*0.70 + profitability*0.20 + cash_generation*0.10) growth = clamp(growth*0.75 + macro_growth*0.25)

Momentum

momentum_score = clamp(trend_score*0.8 + liquidity_score*0.2)

Momentum is currently driven only by price/volume behavior.

Risk

stability_penalty = abs(one_day_return)*1600 + abs(intraday_return)*1000 risk_score = clamp(85 - stability_penalty + liquidity_score*0.15)

With fundamentals + macro, risk adds leverage/cash stress and yield-curve context.

risk = clamp(risk*0.55 + cash_generation*0.15 + leverage*0.30) risk = clamp(risk*0.75 + macro_risk*0.25)

Final Composite Score

The final score is the weighted sum of the five factor scores, then rounded to an integer and clamped to 0-100.

final = round(valuation*0.25 + quality*0.25 + growth*0.20 + momentum*0.20 + risk*0.10)
