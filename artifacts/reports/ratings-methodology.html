Stock Rating Methodology

This page documents how the v6 ratings are calculated in production from the current code path: which features are derived, which data sources feed each feature family, the factor formulas, the weighted composite, and the cross-sectional percentile grading that assigns the final A-F grade relative to the tracked universe.

Rating Scale

Grades are assigned by percentile rank against the tracked universe (AAII A+ style) in even 20% buckets, not by absolute score thresholds. The displayed score is the composite percentile rescaled to 0-100, so roughly 20% of symbols fall in each band. Grades are relative: a symbol can change grade as the universe changes even if its own fundamentals do not.

Factor Calculations

Valuation

liquidity_score = clamp(25 + daily_volume / 200000) reversal_score = clamp(55 - one_day_return*250 - intraday_return*100) valuation = average(reversal/liquidity baseline, earnings_yield, book_to_price, profitability, cash flow, leverage)

If SEC valuation inputs are missing, valuation falls back to the conservative reversal/liquidity baseline.

Quality

quality_baseline = clamp(38 + liquidity*0.45 - abs(intraday_return - one_day_return)*350)

With fundamentals, quality averages the baseline with net margin, cash-flow margin, return on assets, and leverage.

Growth

short_term_trend = clamp(50 + one_day_return*400 + intraday_return*150) growth = average(short_term_trend, revenue_growth_yoy, net_income_growth_yoy, operating_cash_flow_growth_yoy, medium_term_momentum)

Macro yield-curve slope can raise or lower the growth component.

growth = clamp(growth*0.75 + macro_growth*0.25)

Momentum

momentum_score = clamp(50 + best_available_100/60/20/10/5/1_day_return*120 + twenty_day_return*60 + liquidity*0.05)

Momentum prefers longer available lookbacks and falls back to short-term returns when compact provider data is all that exists.

Risk

risk_penalty = abs(one_day_return)*250 + abs(intraday_return)*150 + volatility*450 + max_drawdown*250 risk = average(price_stability, leverage, cash_generation, profitability)

With macro data, risk adds yield-curve context.

risk = clamp(risk*0.75 + macro_risk*0.25)

Final Composite Score

Scoring runs in two passes. Pass 1 (per symbol) computes the weighted composite of the six factor scores. Pass 2 (cross-sectional) ranks every symbol's composite against the whole universe and assigns the A-F grade from its percentile; the displayed score is that percentile rescaled to 0-100.

composite = valuation*0.225 + quality*0.225 + growth*0.18 + momentum*0.18 + risk*0.09 + analyst_revision*0.10 score = round(percentile_rank(composite, universe) * 100)

Benchmark Scores

Three externally-validated, fully-specified value/quality scores are shown alongside the composite as benchmarks. They are deliberately excluded from the weighted composite so they stay directly comparable to their published backtests, and they are diagnostic rather than standalone buy signals.

Piotroski F-Score

0-9: nine binary profitability / leverage / efficiency signals

Shown as N/9. Full-confidence values use all nine signals and are highlighted; partial values are muted and explain their lower confidence in the tooltip. Designed as a second-stage filter on already-cheap stocks.

Magic Formula (Greenblatt)

rank(ROIC) + rank(EBIT / enterprise value); lowest sum = best (rank 1)

Cross-sectional combined rank across the universe; financials and utilities excluded when sector is known.

Acquirer's Multiple (Carlisle)

enterprise value / EBIT

Deep-value lens; lower is cheaper. EBIT is approximated by us-gaap OperatingIncomeLoss.
